Anyway. Hi.

I'm in my final year of a BS-MS in Economics at IIT Roorkee. Mostly I build AI agents and the systems around them, and I like distributed systems, ML performance and cryptography.

Work

  • AgentNet (open source): I wrote skill-fire, which lets coding agents find the right skill mid-task and use it, in Claude Code, Cursor and Hermes. I also wrote most of webagent, AgentNet's agent framework.
  • NoBroker: at ConvoZen I built a multi-agent runtime from scratch on self-hosted open-source LLMs, running their sales and lead pipelines. At NoBroker Interiors I worked on real-time interior previews, implementing a Meta paper on point-cloud analysis.
  • Syndie.io: rebuilt NVIDIA's BigVGAN vocoder for XTTS voice cloning. Speaker similarity went from 0.50 to 0.83, at 13× real time.
  • ProveKit (World Foundation): merged adversarial tests for its MSM constraints, including a forgery attempt.

Projects

  • Anton On-Call: an on-call agent that can only propose a diff. A separate runner tests it and signs a tamper-evident receipt.
  • Anton: four agents (triage, code, test, PR) work each bug in parallel, with a Slack approve/reject loop.
  • Distributed LLM training: Qwen2.5-3B on two 4×A10G spot nodes with FSDP. MFU went from 28% to 46%.
  • PIDS: physics-informed diffusion. Cut the Darcy-flow PDE residual from 38.8 to 0.106.
  • ConsensusKV: a Raft key-value store in Go.
